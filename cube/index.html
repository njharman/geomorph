Geomorph Cube

A 2×2 Rubik's Cube of dungeon map tiles. Turn it and the dungeon rearranges.

How it works

Inspired by this blog.

Imagine the cross below folded up into a cube. Further imagine it as a 2x2 Rubik's Cube where each of the 6 faces can be turned, taking its column or row of tiles with it. Faces 5, 2, 4 and 3 turn their columns (left to right on the map), faces 1 and 6 turn the two rows across the beam. Those can be rotated up or down (which shifts/rotates various other tiles/faces/sides of the cube). If you grasped all that, awesome! Now imagine an evil intelligence has placed a party of characters into this dungeon cube and laughs maniacally whenever (s)he rotates the cube shifting the exit the party was about to reach. That evil intelligence is you, DM.

Use the arrows around the map: the number is the face it turns (press F to show face numbers on the map), and hovering an arrow outlines the 12 tiles it will move. The address bar always holds the current map, so you can bookmark or share it.

Tiles: tick any mix of geomorph sets. Each change, or a press of Deal, picks a random 24 tiles from the ticked sets; the map's arrangement stays the same. Tiles from different sets fit together.

Click any single tile in the flat map to turn just that tile a quarter turn clockwise; undo works on it too.

Keys: 1–6 turn that face's column or row (Shift reverses), Z undo, Y redo, S scramble, R reset, V flat/3D, F face numbers.

Tiles:

Pick geomorph sets, then Deal for a random 24.

Geomorph tiles by Tim Ballew (Risus Monkey). Geomorph format originated by Dyson Logos.
