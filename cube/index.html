Imagine the cross below folded up into a cube. Further imagine it as a 2x2 Rubik's Cube with 6 "columns" of rotation. 1-4 being first four columns left to right. 5-6 being horizontal, the two rows across the beam. Being a Rubik's Cube those columns can be rotated up or down (which shifts/rotates various other tiles/faces/sides of the cube). If you grasped all that, awesome! Now imagine an evil intelligence has placed a party of characters into this dungeon cube and laughs maniacally whenever (s)he rotates the cube shifting the exit the party was about to reach. That evil intelligence is you, DM.
